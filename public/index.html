About this model and its limitations

This is a statistical estimate based on a single dataset, not a medical or counseling diagnosis. Its test accuracy is modest. Do not use it as the sole basis for high-stakes decisions or to label a student.

Predictions are computed locally in this browser. The static model file is downloaded when this page loads.
